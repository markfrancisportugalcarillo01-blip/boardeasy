BoardEasy

Your Boarding House, Made Easier.

A boarding-house discovery and management app that brings students, landlords and parents in Naga City, Philippines onto one platform: finding a room, booking it, paying rent and splitting utility bills fairly, and keeping every record in one place.

Why BoardEasy

Room hunting is scatteredRent, distance to campus, house rules, curfews and safety notes usually have to be gathered from different places before a student can compare options.
Shared meters are hard to splitWhen several tenants share one electricity meter, it is hard for them to see how their share was worked out, or to question it.
Records live on paperReceipts, deposits and the condition of a room at move-in are easy to lose, which makes refunds and disagreements harder to settle.
Parents are far awayFamilies who help pay for a student's room have little visibility of balances and receipts unless the student shares them.
Landlords juggle it all by handRent, vacancies, bookings, announcements and bills are tracked separately, often in notebooks and chat threads.

These are the design premises the app is built around, not findings from a survey.

One platform, four roles

Objectives

  1. Help students compare boarding houses by price, distance to campus, rules, amenities and safety notes, then request a room in the app.
  2. Give landlords one place to manage rooms, tenants, bookings, rent, deposits and announcements.
  3. Make shared electricity and water bills transparent: every share shows its readings, rate and formula.
  4. Keep receipts, room condition reports and deposits on record, and turn on-time payments into a portable Rental Passport.
  5. Let parents follow payments only with the student's permission.

Built for Naga City

The app is set in real Naga City barangays around four real campuses, and it calculates bills with real local utility rates.

Ateneo de Naga UniversityUniversity of Nueva CaceresNaga College FoundationUniversidad de Sta. Isabel

Electricity: CASURECO II residential rate, ₱11.17/kWh (October 2026). Water: Metro Naga Water District residential rates (2026). Rents are estimates from public listings. All boarding houses, people and transactions in the demo are fictional.

How BoardEasy works

Each role sees its own screens, but they all read and write the same records. A booking a student sends becomes a request on the landlord's dashboard; a bill the landlord records becomes a charge on the tenant's account and, with permission, a line on the parent's view.

Value and feasibility

BoardEasy's value comes from putting every party's records in one place, and from showing the working behind each number. The benefits below are what the design is intended to deliver once in real use; the demo shows how each would work.

Students

  • Compare rooms on the things that matter, side by side.
  • Check exactly how a utility share was calculated, and question it.
  • Keep receipts and room records for move-out.
  • Build a Rental Passport from on-time payments.

Landlords

  • See rent collected, overdue accounts and vacancies at a glance.
  • Split shared bills with a formula tenants can verify.
  • Settle deposits with photo evidence.
  • Reach all tenants through announcements.

Parents

  • Follow balances and receipts the student chooses to share.
  • Pay on the student's behalf (simulated in the demo).
  • Read the boarding house's safety notes.

Administrators

  • Review new listings before they are labeled verified.
  • Handle reported listings and reviews.
  • Manage accounts and platform rates.
