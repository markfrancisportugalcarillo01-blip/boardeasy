# BoardEasy

A demo of a boarding house discovery and management app for students, landlords and parents in Naga City, Philippines.

**Open the app:** the GitHub Pages link for this repository. It works on Android phones and on laptops.

## About the demo

- Everything runs in one file, `index.html`, with no server.
- Changes are saved in the browser you use (localStorage), on that device only.
- Demo accounts are on the sign-in screen.

## What is real and what is fictional

- **Real:** Naga City barangays and campus names, the CASURECO II residential electricity rate, and Metro Naga Water District residential water rates.
- **Estimates:** room and bedspace rents, based on public Naga City listings (November 2026).
- **Fictional:** every boarding house, person, review, payment, meter reading, verification badge and flood label. No real money is processed.

## Rate sources

- Electricity: CASURECO II residential rate, ₱11.17/kWh, October 2026, via [PRESYO-PH](https://presyo.icsc.ngo/national) (Institute for Climate and Sustainable Cities).
- Water: [Metro Naga Water District schedule of approved water rates](https://www.mnwd.gov.ph/water-rates/) (LWUA Board Resolution No. 22, s. 2026).
- Rents: [Rent.ph Naga City dormitory listings](https://rent.ph/dormitories-for-rent/naga-city) and student bedspace guides.
