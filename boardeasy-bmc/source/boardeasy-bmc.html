Value Propositions

One app for finding, renting and living in a boarding house.

For students and boarders

  • Compare rooms by rent, distance to campus, house rules, amenities and safety notes, then request a booking in the app.
  • See how every bill share was calculated: readings, rate and formula; question any bill.
  • Receipts, room condition photos and deposits on record; a Rental Passport of on-time payments.

For landlords

  • One dashboard for rooms, tenants, bookings, rent, deposits and announcements.
  • Fair electricity and water splitting with a formula tenants can verify.

For parents

  • See balances and receipts the student allows, and pay on their behalf.

Customer Relationships

  • Self-service: each role works on its own dashboard.
  • Automated: rent reminders, bill alerts and pickup reminders as prioritized notifications.
  • Community: reviews labeled verified only after a recorded stay.
  • Guided: a welcome guide for each role; landlords answer inquiries in the app.
Low cost: mostly automated

Channels

  • Now: the browser prototype on GitHub Pages, on phones and laptops.
  • Planned: an Android app on Google Play and a web app.
  • Campus orientations, student organizations and social media to reach students.
  • Direct visits to boarding houses near the four campuses to sign up landlords.

Customer Segments

A multi-sided platform for a niche, local market.

Students and boarders

Students looking for or renting rooms near Ateneo de Naga University, University of Nueva Caceres, Naga College Foundation and Universidad de Sta. Isabel.

Landlords

Owners and managers of boarding houses, dormitories, apartment-style rooms and bedspaces in Naga City barangays.

Parents and guardians

Families who support a student's stay and want visibility of payments, with the student's permission.

Local service businesses

Possible referral partners serving boarders (proposed).

Most important

Students and landlords: each side brings the other. Landlords are the proposed paying segment; students and parents use it free.

Cost Structure

Lean and value-driven: automation keeps costs low. Proposed first-year seed budget: ₱20,000 (development ₱3,000, infrastructure ₱2,500, pilot ₱11,000, operations ₱3,500).

  • Development: building and maintaining the Android app and web app.
  • Infrastructure: servers, database and storage (the prototype runs on free hosting).
  • Payment processing fees once a real gateway is used.
  • App store registration and publishing.
  • Operations: listing verification, moderation and user support.
  • Marketing and landlord onboarding; data privacy and security compliance.
Fixed: hosting, upkeepVariable: payment fees, support

Revenue Streams

No revenue yet: BoardEasy is a prototype. Proposed streams below use the sample prices in the app's business simulation, which are untested assumptions.

₱25per listed room per month: landlord subscription
₱5per successful in-app payment: transaction fee
₱500per local service partner per month: referral or placement fee
  • Landlords pay for the management tools they use; no charge to students or parents is modeled.
  • Fixed list prices; actual pricing to be validated with Naga City landlords before launch.
