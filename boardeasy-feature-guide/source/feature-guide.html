BoardEasy feature guide

Every feature for students, landlords, parents and administrators, and how each one records its information

Each table lists a role's features, what they do, and, where a feature records information (meter readings, bills, payments, photos and so on), how that information gets into the app. Features that only display information say so.

Everything describes the current browser prototype (version 1.0.0). It stores records in the browser on the device; the planned Android and web apps will use a secure cloud database instead.

Simulated Works in the demo but does not use a real service yet (payments, photo reading, GPS, verification).
External link Opens another company's website; nothing is processed inside BoardEasy.
Automatic The app calculates or creates the record by itself from data already logged.

How readings and records are loggedThe four most important data flows

Electricity meter reading

  1. Who: the landlord, once a month. Path: Utilities → Record electricity bill.
  2. Pick the property and billing month. The previous reading fills in automatically from last month's bill.
  3. Take or upload a meter photo. The app suggests the current reading Simulated and the landlord checks it, or types the reading by hand.
  4. The current reading must be at least the previous one. The rate fills in as CASURECO II's ₱11.17/kWh and can be edited.
  5. Choose the split: submeters (enter each room's previous and current reading; leftover common-area use is paid by the landlord or split by days) or one shared meter (a shared percentage split by days, the rest by days and declared appliances).
  6. Preview every share, then Finalize and send to tenants.

Saved: meter ID, date, photo, readings, rate, method, formula and each tenant's share. Each share becomes a charge and a notification. Edits recalculate shares and keep the old numbers in a change history.

Water bill reading

  1. Who: the landlord. Path: Utilities → Water → Record water bill.
  2. Enter the house meter consumption in m³: the app computes the bill with Metro Naga Water District rates (₱160 for the first 10 m³, then ₱18.40 to ₱24.25 per m³). Or type the total from the actual water bill.
  3. Optionally attach a photo of the bill or meter.
  4. Choose the split: per-room submeters (each room's readings), equal, by days occupied, or custom amounts that must add up exactly to the total.
  5. Finalize to send each tenant their share.

Tenants see the same breakdown and can tap Question this bill. Shares are split to the exact centavo so they always add up to the bill.

Rent and payments

  1. Rent charges are created Automatic when a tenant is checked in, due on the 1st of each month. The landlord can also generate the next month's rent from Rent tracking.
  2. In-app payment: the student or a connected parent taps Pay, chooses GCash, Maya, Demo Wallet or Manual, and can pay part of a bill Simulated.
  3. Cash or outside payment: the landlord opens the charge and uses Record payment: amount received, method and date received.

Every attempt is logged as a transaction with a reference number (for example DEMO-2026-10001), amount, method, date and status: paid, failed or cancelled. Already-paid bills cannot be paid twice. A receipt is created for each payment.

Room condition and deposits

  1. Move-in or move-out report (landlord: Room reports; student: My rental): date, room photos, furniture condition (Good, Fair, Worn, Damaged), appliance condition, and existing damage.
  2. Move-out adds cleaning condition, changes from the original condition, and up to three proposed deductions with amounts.
  3. The other party confirms the report; finalized reports are never overwritten, only revised with history.
  4. Deposit: its amount is recorded at check-in; the landlord enters deductions (label and amount, never more than the deposit) and records the refund Simulated.

Photos are resized in the browser before saving. Students can compare move-in and move-out side by side.
